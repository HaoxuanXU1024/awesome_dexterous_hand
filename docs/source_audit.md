# Sources and scope

This list covers three focused directions:

- Dexterous-hand reinforcement learning.
- Unified latent or action spaces for dexterous hands.
- Dexterous hands with tactile or force sensing.

Papers are grouped by the direction that best matches their main contribution. Adjacent methods are included when their latent, policy-learning, or tactile component is directly useful for dexterous manipulation.
